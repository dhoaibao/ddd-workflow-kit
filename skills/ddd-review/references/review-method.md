# Lean review method

## Scope first

Name one increment, authority set, acceptance criteria, evidence boundary, and allowed review path. Inventory exact artifacts and report missing/stale paths without reconstruction.

## Run gates internally

Check fit, evidence/provenance, lifecycle/schema, vocabulary, strategic/tactical prerequisites, adoption safety, cross-artifact consistency, utility, and authority revisions. Summarize passing gates in one sentence; emit only material exceptions.

## Queue and routing

Every issue receives increment impact, disposition, owner, smallest action, affected paths, and revisit trigger. Route discovery evidence to discovery, boundaries/language to strategic, behavior/invariants to tactical, delivery safety to adoption, and review completeness to review. Mark later artifacts stale when authority changes.

## Readiness

`documentation_readiness` describes document coherence. `increment_gate` is `blocked` or `awaiting-ratification`. The latter requires clear authority, acceptance, containment, target/runtime, owner, and no material findings but still does not authorize implementation. Human ratification is a separate orchestrator action.
