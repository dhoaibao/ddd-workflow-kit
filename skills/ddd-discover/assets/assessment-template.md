# DDD fit assessment

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| `scope` | _one project, capability, or increment_ |
| `state` | _working, decision-needed, current, stale, or superseded_ |

## Decision-bearing outcome

- **Outcome:** _one desired outcome._
- **In scope:** _capability/slice._
- **Out of scope:** _explicit exclusions._
- **Mode:** _greenfield, brownfield, or mixed._

## Fit decision

- **Decision:** _fit, limited-fit, or not-fit._
- **Evidence:** _concrete rules, ambiguity, change risk, integration, or simpler CRUD evidence._
- **Simpler path:** _required for not-fit._
- **Revisit signal:** _evidence that changes the decision._

## Material evidence and next action

| Claim type | Material claim | Evidence/owner | Impact on increment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| _current behavior, desired policy/meaning, obligation, interpretation, proposal, or assumption_ | _claim_ | _source_ | _why it changes the next action_ |

- **Next action:** _one owner action or bounded stop._
