# DDD discovery assessment

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| `artifact` | `assessment` |
| `status` | `draft` |
| `validation` | `unvalidated` |
| `owner` | `ddd-discover` |
| `scope` | _project, domain, or capability_ |
| `provenance` | _sources, contributors, and dates_ |
| `assumptions` | _explicit assumptions or none recorded_ |
| `open_questions` | _unresolved questions or none recorded_ |
| `last_updated` | _YYYY-MM-DD_ |

## Desired outcomes

- **Outcome:** _what success means and for whom._
- **In scope:** _capability or workflow._
- **Out of scope:** _explicit exclusions._

## Current-system mode

- **Mode:** _greenfield, brownfield, or mixed._
- **Observed behavior:** _current behavior supported by tests, operations, contracts, or other evidence._
- **Seams, dependencies, and ownership:** _especially for brownfield work._

## Fit assessment

- **Decision:** _fit, limited-fit, or not-fit._
- **Complexity signals:** _rules, ambiguity, change risk, differentiation, integration, or costly mistakes._
- **Simpler alternative:** _required when not-fit; optional otherwise._
- **Revisit signal:** _evidence that could change the decision._

## Evidence table

| Claim type | Claim | Source/owner/date | Validation | Impact |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| _current behavior, desired policy/meaning, obligation, interpretation, proposal, or assumption_ | _claim_ | _source_ | _unvalidated, partial, or validated_ | _why it matters_ |

## Constraints and open questions

- **Constraints:** _privacy, ownership, access, delivery, or integration constraints._
- **Open questions:** _material decisions or missing evidence._

## Next-stage recommendation

- **Recommendation:** _bounded stop, focused discovery, or `ddd-strategic`._
- **Handoff objective:** _one bounded objective and required evidence bundle._
- **Stop condition:** _what must be answered before continuing._
